Privacy Policy

Effective Date / Last Updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Crystalia Gem Legends ("the App", "we", "us", "our") — developed for the Google Play package com.crystaliagem.legmee — handles information when you use the App. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

1. Information We Collect

Crystalia: Gem Legends is designed to be played fully offline. The App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to play.

All gameplay data — including your score, coin balance, level progress, unlocked power-ups, daily reward streak, and app settings (sound, music, vibration, theme, graphics quality) — is stored locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

The App does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Precise or approximate location data
  • Photos, contacts, or files from your device
  • Payment or financial information
  • Device identifiers used for tracking or advertising purposes

2. In-App Leaderboard

The leaderboard feature displayed in the App is generated and stored locally on your device for entertainment purposes. It does not connect to an external server and does not share your score or identity with other players or the public.

3. Advertising and Analytics

Crystalia: Gem Legends does not display third-party advertisements and does not integrate any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. No behavioral or usage data is collected for advertising purposes.

4. Permissions

The App may request minimal on-device permissions (such as vibration for haptic feedback) that are strictly used to support core gameplay features and are never used to collect or share personal data.

5. Children's Privacy

Crystalia: Gem Legends does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region). Since the App does not collect personal data and functions entirely offline, it is safe for players of all ages.

6. Data Storage and Security

Because all data remains on your device, you retain full control over it. You can permanently delete all App data at any time by using the "Reset Progress" option within the App's Settings menu, or by clearing the App's storage/uninstalling the App through your device's system settings.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share any data with third parties, because no personal or gameplay data leaves your device.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App's functionality or for legal and regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
